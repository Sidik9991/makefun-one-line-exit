# Avoiding Us

A one-line resignation letter for make.fun's "The One-Line Exit" bounty.

**The entry:**

> I quit. After nine years as your lighthouse keeper, I've realized the ships were never lost — they were avoiding us.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node, no dependencies) holds ten candidate one-line resignation
letters, each scored 0–2 on the judge's five criteria — joke lands (×5),
surprise (×4), fits the form (×3), specific voice (×2), quotable (×1). The
highest scorer wins at runtime; ties go to the shorter line. The program
writes the winner to `/output/entry.txt`, re-reads it, and validates the
gate exactly: UTF-8, 1–140 characters, not only white space, exactly one
line — failing loudly otherwise.
